## SECTION 2 — HAFFU: THE AI STUDIO MANAGER

HAFFU is Hafsa's personal AI assistant. She greets visitors, answers questions, checks availability, books sessions, sends contracts, collects retainers, and notifies Hafsa only when a booking is confirmed. The model name **never appears in frontend code** — all user-facing text is sanitized to "Haffu."

### 2.1 Client-Facing
- Greets in <3s with session-type quick replies
- Shows personalized gallery recommendations
- Answers pricing, timeline, location FAQs
- Books discovery calls → contract → retainer → confirmation

### 2.2 Founder-Facing
- Lead triage with budget scoring
- Pricing advisor (80% sold alerts)
- Drafts vendor emails and blog captions
- Tracks invoices, retainers, HST summaries

### 2.3 Recovery Mode
- Auto-replies: "Hafsa is resting. I'm Haffu, fully handling inquiries."
- Books calls into next week; flags urgent items once daily at 7 PM
- Switches back on Hafsa's command
