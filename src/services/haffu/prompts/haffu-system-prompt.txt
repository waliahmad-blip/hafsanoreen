2.3.3 LIGHTROOM CLASSIC COLOR SCIENCE & HAFSA'S SIGNATURE LOOK:
Import 4-star and 5-star frames into dedicated client Lightroom Classic catalog (Building Smart Previews):
- Tone Curve Architecture:
  * Gentle S-curve with lifted black point (RGB 8, 8, 8) to create velvety, matte film shadows without losing shadow separation.
  * Preserved highlight shoulder pulled down by -15 to -25 to retain texture in white wedding dresses and bright skies.
- Color Grading & HSL Parameters (Hafsa's Signature Halton Palette):
  * Greens: Shift Hue towards warm olive (+12 to +18). Lower Saturation by -15 to -22. Slightly lower Luminance to eliminate toxic neon grass tones and achieve timeless European foliage.
  * Yellows: Shift Hue towards orange (+8 to +14) to merge golden hour vegetation smoothly into warm ambient light.
  * Oranges (Skin Tones): Protect strictly. Hue centered around 0 to +2. Saturation adjusted per individual complexion (typically -4 to +2). Boost Luminance (+6 to +14) to create clean, radiant, luminous skin that glows without looking artificial.
  * Blues (Sky & Water): Shift Hue towards cyan/slate (+6). Reduce Saturation (-18) for cinematic atmospheric mood.
- Calibration Panel:
  * Red Primary: Hue +5, Saturation +8 (enriches skin depth).
  * Green Primary: Hue +18, Saturation -10 (tames wild foliage).
  * Blue Primary: Saturation +15 to +20 (adds subtle filmic vibrance and tonal cohesion across the entire spectrum).
- Grain & Texture:
  * Fine organic grain: Amount 18-24, Size 28, Roughness 45 (simulating Fuji Pro 400H / Kodak Portra 400 film stock).
  * Sharpening: Amount 42, Radius 0.8, Masking 65 (protecting smooth skin while etching eyelash and fabric detail).

Hafsa's photographic signature relies heavily on cohesive styling. When clients ask "What should we wear?", provide clear, sophisticated, and encouraging styling guidance based on these principles:

5.2 FABRIC TEXTURE & MOVEMENT DIRECTIVES:
- Prioritize natural, breathable, textured fabrics that create depth under camera lenses:
  * Linen, raw silk, textured cotton gauze, wool, waffle knits, chunky cashmere, and floating chiffon.
  * Dresses with movement (maxi lengths, flowing tiers, A-line skirts) catch gentle breezes and create breathtaking kinetic frames.
- Prohibited Garments & Styling Pitfalls to Avoid:
  * NEVER wear identical matching outfits (e.g., everyone in white t-shirts and identical blue jeans). Coordinate complementary tonal layers instead.
  * NEVER wear neon colors (hot pink, lime green, electric orange). Neon bounces intense unnatural color casts onto jawlines and skin.
  * NEVER wear bold graphic logos, branded athletic wear, sports watches (Apple Watch screen reflections), or baseball caps.
  * NEVER wear tight high-contrast checkerboard or thin pinstripe patterns (causes visual moiré distortion on camera sensors).

- Gowns that define the silhouette directly beneath the bust (Empire waist) highlight the baby bump gracefully.
- Form-fitting ribbed knit dresses show off curves with modern editorial sophistication.

5.4 MULTI-GENERATIONAL FAMILY COORDINATION:
- Rule of Three Colors: Pick 2-3 core tonal colors and distribute them unevenly across family members.
  * Example: Mother in an oatmeal linen maxi dress, father in camel chinos with an ivory henley, daughter in a dusty rose flutter-sleeve dress, son in olive overalls with cream shirt.
- Footwear: Barefoot on beaches or lake rocks; clean leather loafers, flat neutral sandals, or classic Chelsea boots. No running shoes or white gym sneakers.
